I've read both documents in full — ARCHITECTURE.md §9, §11, §13, §13.2a, §13.5, §15, §21.2, ADR-2/ADR-8, and PRD §8.6, §9 (BR-21/28/29/32/33), §17.5 (NFR-DAT-05/08), SC-55/56/57, SEC-R3, EC-23/25. Before scoring, the specific cracks I found by tracing the proposal against those sections:

**Where it breaks or nearly breaks:**

1. **Variant A (BIGINT) is a conceded BR-21 violation that is still on the table.** SQLSTATE 22003 at 2^63−1 halalas is a rejection for size (BR-21, SEC-R3, EC-25), with no legal way to report it under ARCH 13.5's eight-reason contract. The proposal says so itself — but still presents A first, labeled "the classic." For three vibe coders skimming an S0-12 decision, offering a non-compliant variant is a loaded footgun. It must be struck, not argued against.

2. **A latent bug in the recommended Variant B's own sketch:** `v_amount_halalas := p_amount_sar * 100` in PostgreSQL numeric arithmetic yields `10001.00` (scale is preserved: 2+0), and a **bare** NUMERIC column stores that scale. The trunc CHECK passes (`10001.00 = trunc(10001.00)` is true in numeric equality), so `current_price_halalas::TEXT` emits `"10001.00"` — and the prescribed `BigInt("10001.00")` formatter **throws**. The accept/reject math is unaffected (numeric comparison ignores scale), but the proposal's own text-boundary contract breaks unless `sar_to_halalas` normalizes with `trunc()`. Unfixed, the "airtight" display path fails on the very first bid.

3. **The 13.5 race distinction is client-cooperative, not guaranteed.** `outbid_race` fires only when the optional `p_seen_price_sar` is supplied; a caller that omits it (SC-43's direct-request suite, or a lazy client) collapses the race into `not_above_current`. Validity is unaffected (BR-08 respected), but "must be distinguishable" becomes conditional on the client volunteering data.

4. **The 2^53 display cliff arrives 100× sooner than the SAR-NUMERIC competitor** (~9×10^13 SAR), and Realtime payloads cannot be view-laundered — the mitigation (treat realtime amounts as untrusted, refetch via a text view) is pure discipline, exactly the resource this team lacks. Silent display corruption at scale is an NFR-DAT-05 violation ("precision must not degrade at scale") that fails without an error.

5. **The unit asymmetry (RPC in SAR, storage in halalas, two conversion sites) recreates the G-1 hazard** ARCH §21.2 exists to prevent, mitigated only by a shared helper nobody is forced to use.

**What genuinely holds:** the SC-55/SC-56 accept/reject trace is bit-exact (10000 ≥ 10000; 10000 > 10000 false; 100.01 × 100 = 10001 in exact numeric — no float anywhere server-side); the two-branch BR-28 comparison matches ARCH 13.2a verbatim with none of the four forbidden checks; fractional halalas are unrepresentable, making NFR-DAT-05 structural; the refusal of NUMERIC(38,0) is exactly the right BR-21 reasoning; and — importantly — because ADR-2 denies direct insert and all validity decisions live in `place_bid`, the worst case of every misuse above is a **wrong display, never a wrong auction outcome**. That floor is real, and it is why developerErrorResistance isn't lower. But the proposal's own honesty is the verdict: the margin over NUMERIC(·,2) SAR is "real but thin, paid with a permanent 100x-error hazard," and it concedes that without money-module discipline "this approach is worse than its competitor." This team is the definition of that condition.
